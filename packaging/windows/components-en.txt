Playback components

Grajek needs two free tools to play YouTube audio. Setup will download them now from their authors' official GitHub releases, verify their SHA-256 checksums and store them in Grajek's installation folder. They do not change system settings or PATH.

yt-dlp {ytdlp_version} ({ytdlp_size} bytes): reads YouTube video and playlist information and audio stream addresses. Includes yt-dlp-ejs {ejs_version} for YouTube challenges. License: GPLv3+.
Source: {ytdlp_url}

Deno {deno_version} ({deno_size} bytes): JavaScript runtime that runs those challenge scripts. License: MIT.
Source: {deno_url}

An internet connection is required. Components are updated with new Grajek releases. If YouTube changes its protections, playback may stop working until a new Grajek release.
